Identity in headers, tables, approval trails and learner lists.

```jsx
<Avatar name="Rahmat Hidayat" size="md" ring />
<Avatar name="Siti Aminah" size="sm" tone="orange" />
```

- The tinted initial IS the default — the Academy and admin console render people this way, not with illustrations or photos.
- One initial on a 10%-alpha brand wash. Never a grey silhouette icon.
- `ring` marks the signed-in user only. Do not use the APPI mascot faces in `assets/appi/` as a person avatar.
