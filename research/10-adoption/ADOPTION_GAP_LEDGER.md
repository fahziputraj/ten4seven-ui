This ledger records actual proof gaps and harness findings. A missing domain-specific feature is not promoted into the ten4seven core without evidence that the contract itself is insufficient.

| GAP-03 | The two-consumer Vite harness initially passed the host flag through an extra separator, which caused a startup timeout in the first run.                      | Harness configuration gap | Normalize the web-server commands to `vite --host 127.0.0.1`; rerun the suite.                                                                         | Closed          |

## Core decision

No component, token, block, or icon catalog change was required by these two consumers. The gap ledger therefore does not create a new `Commerce*` or operational primitive family, and it does not add domain nouns to the core catalog.
